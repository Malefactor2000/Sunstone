Sunstone: where its data comes from, and under which licences
==============================================================

OneForAll by Smoergi: dungeons, loot, recipes, hunter pets and more. Used with the author's permission (GPL-3.0).
Questie / QuestieDB: quests, creatures, items. Read live from the installed addon; no copy of its database is part of this addon. Places and
names in some tables (travel, pets, collections, NPCs) passed through it. Questie's team is fine with copying under the GPL-3.0 and is gathering
its contributors' consent; some old contributions have no licence of their own. With thanks to everyone who worked on it.
HereBeDragons by Nevcairiel (libs/HereBeDragons, unchanged; BSD), with LibStub (public domain) and CallbackHandler-1.0 (BSD).
CMaNGOS classic-db: Classic loot tables, prices, quests and where creatures stand (GPL-3.0); none of the game's own quest texts.
The numbers of pet skills, pet families, spells and talents, and which 3D model is whose: the game's own files (numbers only; models and pictures
are shown from the player's own game, none are shipped). A pet's and an ordinary enemy's numbers by level: CMaNGOS classic-db. Which beasts teach the
bats' and foxes' skills, training points and how many skills a pet can have: Petopia's Forever pages (facts, in our own words).
The game's own files: which side creatures are on, where exploring counts, how Blackfathom Deeps' rooms lie, which pictures make up a zone's map (facts and
file numbers; no pictures are shipped).
Zone outlines (media, zones): drawn for Sunstone from which zone each patch of the game's own terrain belongs to; shapes only.
Campsites: facts from guides for Forever (Warcraft Tavern, wow-professions.com, Method, Gfinity, the Warcraft Wiki), in our own words.
SimGear Forever: stat weights (GPL-3.0). ProfessionDB: recipes (MIT).

Forever Simple World Map 0.30.2 by Tarolg (CurseForge project 1728535): how the rooms of the dungeon maps are laid out (which of the game's
own minimap pictures goes where, boss and entrance spots). The pictures themselves belong to the game and are not part of this addon.
