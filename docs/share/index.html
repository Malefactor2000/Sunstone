Share your finds

Sunstone learns from what its players see: what creatures drop, XP at every level, vendor prices, and where pets and mounts nobody has found yet come from. Sharing is off until you switch it on in the game, and any of the three ways below is up to you. Sunstone works the same if you never share.

1. By hand

In the game, press Share finds at the top of Sunstone's window and say Yes. From then on the button says Send finds: it gives you one link. Paste it into your browser's address bar and you land here with your finds filled in.

2. By itself

A small program for Windows sits by the clock and sends your finds after you log out, at most twice a day, only while sharing is on in the game. Right-click it to read the text first, pause, or delete what you've sent.

  1. In the game, press Share finds at the top of Sunstone's window, then Yes and By itself.
  2. Get SunstoneShare.exe from the latest release, and run it. Windows may say it doesn't know the program: it's free and not signed. Its whole source is one file you can read.
  3. Tick Start with Windows in its menu if you want that.

To remove it: untick Start with Windows, Quit, delete the file. Nothing else is installed.

3. The AI version

Optional, for those who'd like to ask questions about their character: a desktop helper with an AI that runs on your own PC, offline. It can send your finds by itself too, with a switch in its Find tab (off until you turn it on).

What it needs and how to set it up

All free: Windows 10 or 11 and about 10 GB of space; the Questie and OneForAll addons (the program reads their data); a graphics card with 8 GB or more makes the AI quick, without one it's slow.

  1. Install Python 3.13 (tick "Add python.exe to PATH"), and for the AI chat Ollama, the program that runs the AI on your own PC.
  2. Get Sunstone-with-AI from the latest release, close WoW, unzip it and double-click Set up Sunstone. It says what it's going to get before it gets it; the AI model is about 5 GB.
  3. Start WoW, open Sunstone from the desktop shortcut, and type /sun sync in game once. Ctrl+Alt+L shows or hides it.
  4. To share by itself: say Yes to Share finds in game, then tick Send my finds by itself at the bottom of its Find tab.

To remove it: delete the Sunstone folder in your user folder and the shortcut, and uninstall Ollama if you don't use it for anything else.

What's in it, and what never is

  • In it: counts and averages of what your characters saw: kills, coins, drops, XP, fight times, quest rewards, vendor prices, chests, fishing, battlegrounds, dungeon runs; places to a hundredth of a map; names of creatures Sunstone didn't know; a random number standing for "the same sender".
  • Never: your character's, realm's, guild's or account's name, your gold, bags or gear, other players, chat, or anything about your PC.
  • The collector keeps no IP addresses (a fingerprint that changes every day only counts how many shares came in, and is deleted the next day).
  • A newer share of yours replaces the older one. Stopping in the game throws your sender number away.
  • What everyone sends is pooled, checked against everyone else's, and comes back to all players with the next update. A number one player saw isn't used as a chance until others have seen it too.
